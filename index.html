Lull-9 Skimmer

Orrin Foundry built the Lull-9 for tidal flats. It floats eleven centimetres above whatever it is parked over: sand, slate or standing water.

Its ring fans never rev. They hum, and the hull settles into the note. The wings are not for speed; they keep the nose honest when the tide wind turns.
